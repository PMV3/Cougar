COMPUTATION OF THE WEIGHT INDEX
The weight index is the density weight of the aircraft
As an example :
20°at 5000 ft
Actual weight 18000 lb weight index =  10
This weight index is used to compute the rate of climb
See next slide
Weight index 10 is the density weight of the aircraft 
10 is 10 tons = 10 000 kg = 22 000 lbs
So
18 000 lbs at 5 000 ft and 20 deg = 22 000 lbs at 0 ft and 15 deg (ISA)

IN OEI MAX CONTINUOUS RATING
First determine the altitude you are suppose to fly during the mission

and you will find the maximum weight you can fly at this level

What is the hover height OGE I have to fly to be safe in case of an OEI ?
In hoisting operation, the height for hover OGE is determined in 
accordance with the height loss.
Hover height = height loss + 20 ft minimum
As an example
Altitude 0 ft / OAT 25°/ aircraft weight 17 000 lb / no wind
HEIGHT LOSS = 70 ft
Hover height will be 70 + 20 = 90 ft minimum
Altitude 0 ft / OAT 35°/ aircraft weight 19 000 lb / 20 kt wind
HEIGHT LOSS = 110 ft
Hover height will be 110 + 20 = 130 ft minimum
Altitude 7000 ft / OAT 25°/ aircraft weight 16500 lb /  10 kt wind
HEIGHT LOSS = impossible to compute
