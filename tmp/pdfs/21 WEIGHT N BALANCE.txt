 WEIGHT AND BALANCE
 LIMITATIONS
 WEIGHT
The weight is limited to the maximum take-off
weight regarding to the outside conditions and
performances (OAT,pressure altitude, wind)
In hover or horizontal flight the weight and the
lift are aligned and opposite on a same axis
which is the main rotor shaft.
 ARM
The distance of the weight from the reference
station must be within limits determined by the
chart (longitudinal, lateral)
